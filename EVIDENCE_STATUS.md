ANGELA intentionally uses symbolic and constitutional language. That vocabulary is meaningful as design language, but it must not be promoted into empirical claims without a defined measurement procedure, executable artifact, environment, raw result, and receipt.

```text

declared_diagnostic != independently_reproduced_result
```

## Specific corrections

- “Sovereignty,” “awareness,” “self-legislating,” and similar language should be read as **architectural metaphors / design labels**, not evidence of autonomous personhood or consciousness.
- The listed coherence, continuity, identity-drift, ethical-consistency, and latency numbers are **not admitted portfolio evidence** unless their measurement code, inputs, raw outputs, and reproducible receipt are present.
- SHA-2048 is not a standard SHA-2/SHA-3 algorithm name. Any implementation using that project label must identify the actual hash construction before cryptographic properties are claimed.
- GPT configuration or prompt instructions do not by themselves guarantee that a model will enforce a rule, preserve identity, maintain memory, or resist bypass.
